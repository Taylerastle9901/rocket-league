Rocket League

The game where cars play soccer, and it somehow works perfectly.

What Is Rocket League?

Rocket League is a vehicle soccer game. Instead of running players, each team drives rocket-powered cars around an enclosed arena and tries to hit a giant ball into the other team's goal. Matches usually last five minutes, and the most common mode is 3v3, although 1v1 and 2v2 are popular too.

Why I Like It

I have played Rocket League for years, I stream it on Twitch, and I play competitively with the Weber State Esports club. What keeps me coming back is that the game is easy to learn but very hard to master. Anyone can drive around and hit the ball, but the best players can fly through the air and control the ball like it is glued to their car.

Skills Every Player Should Learn

  1. Boost management: collecting small boost pads so you never run empty.
  2. Rotation: knowing when to attack and when to fall back on defense.
  3. Aerials: jumping and boosting to hit the ball in the air.
  4. Dribbling: carrying the ball on the roof of your car.
  5. Flip resets: an advanced trick where touching the ball with all four wheels gives you another flip.

Tip: Go to free play and practice for ten minutes before you queue for ranked.
